Fixed text after the cursor being lost when you split a note's paragraph and left the note straight away.
Fixed Ctrl+Z in a note emptying the paragraph, losing a paragraph you had just joined, or bringing back text that had moved to another paragraph.
Fixed a $$ or two maths formulas side by side ($a$$b$) in a note merging every later paragraph into one.
In a note, Delete at the end of a paragraph joins the next one onto it, Tab no longer moves to the Back button, and Page Up/Down scroll the page after Esc.
Fixed clicking a heading in Contents while editing jumping to the wrong place, and Make Card from a selection starting with a heading now uses that heading as the front.
Fixed maths ending in \\ (a new line) just before the closing $ showing as plain text.
Fixed AltGr characters such as ł, ², µ and { triggering formatting shortcuts instead of typing on Windows.
Fixed Ctrl+P adding an extra bullet point after Ctrl+L, adding a second bullet to lines that already had one, and failing to remove bullets.
Fixed Ctrl+B and the other formatting shortcuts double-wrapping a selection that already included its markers, and Ctrl+M turning display maths inline or merging formulas.
Study days are now recorded in your own time zone, so the calendar and "last studied" are no longer a day off late at night or early in the morning.
Last Day now means today, and Last Week, Last 2 Weeks and the other periods cover exactly that many days including today.
Study time is now saved even when you quit a review before answering anything.
Fixed best answer streaks going to the wrong set when several sets are reviewed together.
Skip is hidden once a card's answer is shown, and Retry Later is hidden when only one card is left.
Closing the deck editor with an unsaved card now asks before discarding it.
Fixed a set's name being saved empty, Match card placeholder text being saved onto other card types, and the formatting toolbar editing a hidden field.
Stats, card counts and sorting now update straight away after editing a set or group, deleting stats or restoring a backup.
"Recently studied" now sorts notes by when you last studied them, not when you last edited them.
Restoring a backup without stats no longer moves your current stats onto different sets.
Settings are saved safely, so a crash while saving can no longer reset them or sign you out.
Signing out now signs out only this device, and also ends the session on the server when your sign-in had expired.
Fixed double-clicking Upload in the Cloud Manager creating duplicate cloud sets.
A failed password reset can be retried without a new code, and closing the window part way through no longer leaves you signed in.
The Cloud Manager and Check for Updates now say when they can't connect, instead of showing an empty list or claiming you're up to date.
Being offline no longer asks you to sign in again when you're already signed in.
Anki import: nested cloze deletions, lists with paragraphs inside, superscripts and subscripts containing maths, literal braces and backslashes, and cards in filtered decks now come across correctly.
Anki import is much faster on fields with broken HTML.
Typed cloze answers with several blanks accept commas or spaces between them, ignore formatting, and empty blanks no longer count as blanks.
The editor preview updates as you type, and only waits for a short pause in typing on cards with enough maths to make it slow, so long cards stay smooth to type in.
Rename boxes are limited to 100 characters, and their OK button is disabled while the name is blank.
Fixed a crash opening the Cloud Manager or Community sets when signing in took a while, for example just after reconnecting.
Online actions show a spinner while they run, and the Community, Cloud Manager and Sign In buttons can no longer be clicked again while ReviFlash Online is checking your sign-in.
Search waits for a short pause in typing, so typing a word no longer redraws the library on every letter.
Fixed long names on library cards being cut off at a word with no "…".
F or Ctrl+F jumps to search, and the arrow keys move between library cards; Tab no longer moves around the main window.
E, M, S and Delete edit, move, show stats for or delete the highlighted card, and Ctrl+N creates a set, note, folder or group.
Esc always backs out a step: it closes Settings, Help, Login, the card editor and the cloud windows, leaves a note, clears the search, leaves a folder, and on the main menu asks before quitting.
F1 shows every keyboard shortcut, and on the main menu F2 opens Settings and F3 opens the graph view.
The graph view shows a spinner while its charts load.
In a note, Tab shows or hides the contents and Ctrl+Enter makes a card, and the Contents button is no longer coloured when the contents are showing.
Time spent editing sets is tracked like notes time (only while you're working, pausing after 2 minutes idle), including making cards from notes, and shows as Editing Time in stats, the study time chart and the calendar.
Folders have a stats button (or S) showing the same stats as overall for everything inside them, and your best streak sits beside the current one at the top.
Every answer in a review is recorded against its card (which cloze blank or side, right or wrong, and how long it took), kept in backups, ready for per-card stats.
New \T{...} code style (Ctrl+T or the toolbar) shows text in a typewriter font, also inside maths, and maths supports \texttt{...} and \textsf{...} with their spaces kept. Anki code formatting comes across as \T.
The deck editor shows a spinner while its cards load, and the Front and Back boxes grow with what you type.
Cards in the deck editor have a Move or Copy button: moving takes the card to another set with its answer history, and copying adds a fresh copy to any set, including the same one.
The deck editor's Duplicate button is now New Card From This, which starts a new card from that one with the cursor in the Front box.
Fixed the deck editor asking before discarding an empty card, for example after clearing a card you were editing or changing the card type.
The Cloud Manager's private and public icons are redrawn so they no longer look squashed.
Help has a new Changelog tab listing the changes in each version, and it opens by itself after an update (Settings → Updates to turn that off).
Images in cards and notes: paste one with Ctrl+V, drag an image file in, or use the new Image button. Each sits on its own line at its own size (smaller if it doesn't fit), and \img[50%]{...} makes it that share of the width.
An image in a cloze blank stays hidden until the answer is shown; a typed cloze can't hide an image.
Images are included in backups, and images no card or note uses any more are removed when ReviFlash starts.
Anki import brings images across instead of marking them [image]; only images missing from the file or in a format ReviFlash can't read are still marked.
Sets with images can't be uploaded to the cloud yet, and Upload says why.
A new app icon, sharp at every size on Windows, macOS and Linux.
Notes can go online like sets: the Cloud Manager and Community windows have a Sets / Notes switch to upload a note privately or publicly, update or delete it, and download your own or other people's notes.
Notes with images can't be uploaded yet, the same as sets.
