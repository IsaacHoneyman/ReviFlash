=== 1.4.1
\H2{Themes}
- A new \B{Themes} window, opened from Settings, shows every theme as a tile in its own colours.
- Make your own: pick a background and two accents, and ReviFlash works out the rest so text stays readable. Change any single colour if you like, name it, and keep up to 12. They're saved in your backups.
- Seventeen themes were retired, including the light themes. If you were using one, you're now on Vaporwave.

\H2{A cleaner main menu}
- Every card has the same kind of line under its name: when a set was last studied, a group's cards, a folder's contents or a note's last edit.
- Notes have their own colour, and their badge shows the word count.
- Badges are readable on every theme.

\H2{Clearer messages}
- When something goes wrong, a message pops up with the whole explanation instead of a cut-off line in the corner.
- Confirmations and errors have a short title, like "Delete Set?", with the details underneath.
- Success messages show briefly, then fade.

\H2{Everywhere else}
- Everything you can reach with Tab or the arrow keys now shows a clear highlight.
- Text sizes, colours and capitalisation are the same in every window, and the app says "set" everywhere.
- Community and the Cloud Manager switch between sets and notes with a sliding switch.
- \B{Delete All Stats} now clears notes time and your launch streak too.
- Typed answers ignore extra spaces, and grades always match the percentage shown.
- Over 20 more fixes.

=== 1.4.0
\H2{Images}
- Add images to cards and notes: paste one with Ctrl+V, drag an image file in, or use the new \B{Image} button.
- Images sit on their own line, and a size like [50%] makes one that share of the width.
- An image in a cloze blank stays hidden until the answer is shown.
- Images are kept in backups, and Anki import now brings images across.
- Sets with images can't be uploaded to the cloud yet.

\H2{Notes in the cloud}
- Upload notes like sets: the Cloud Manager and Community have a \B{Sets / Notes} switch.
- Keep a note private to move it between your own computers, or share it publicly for others to find and download.

\H2{Keyboard shortcuts everywhere}
- On the main menu, the arrow keys move between cards, F or Ctrl+F jumps to search and Ctrl+N creates a set, note, folder or group.
- E, M, S and Delete edit, move, show stats for or delete the highlighted card.
- Esc always backs out a step, and on the main menu asks before quitting.
- F1 shows every shortcut, F2 opens Settings and F3 opens the graph view.
- In a note, Tab shows or hides the contents and Ctrl+Enter makes a card.

\H2{Deck editor}
- Move or copy a card to any set. Moving keeps its answer history; copying makes a fresh card, even in the same set.
- \B{New Card From This} starts a new card from an existing one.
- The Front and Back boxes grow as you type, and a spinner shows while cards load.
- The preview updates as you type, and only waits for a pause on cards with lots of maths.
- It only asks before discarding a card when there's something to lose.

\H2{Stats}
- Time spent editing sets is tracked like notes time, and shows as Editing Time in stats, the study time chart and the calendar.
- Folders have their own stats button, covering everything inside them.
- Your best answer streak sits beside your current one.
- Every answer is now recorded against its card, ready for per-card stats.
- Study days are recorded in your own time zone, and Last Day, Last Week and the other periods cover exactly that many days including today.

\H2{Formatting}
- A new code style (Ctrl+T or the toolbar) shows text in a typewriter font, also inside maths.
- Many fixes to formatting shortcuts, bullet points, maths and undo in notes.

\H2{Online}
- Online actions show a spinner while they run, and say clearly when they can't connect.
- Signing out only signs out this device, and being offline no longer asks you to sign in again.

\H2{Also}
- A new app icon, sharp at every size.
- A changelog in Help, which opens after each update (you can turn that off in Settings).
- Anki import handles nested clozes, lists, braces and filtered decks better, and is much faster on messy fields.
- Typed cloze answers with several blanks accept commas or spaces between them.
- Over 50 other fixes, from search speed to settings that can no longer be lost in a crash.

=== 1.3.0
\H2{Lecture notes}
- Write notes next to your flashcards from \B{+ New → Note}, with the same formatting, headings and maths as cards.
- Click a paragraph to edit it; notes save themselves as you type.
- A Contents panel lists the note's headings, and \B{Make Card} turns selected text into a card in any set.

\H2{Stats}
- Time in notes is counted, and overall stats show Total Time split into flashcards and notes.
- Every note has its own stats, and the graph view gains Where Your Time Went and a Study Calendar.

\H2{Also}
- Headings in notes and cards, with H1–H3 toolbar buttons and Ctrl+1/2/3.
- A Help button with the Formatting Guide and a Keyboard Shortcuts list.
- ReviFlash opens about 2.5 times faster, and the background swirl uses far less CPU.

=== 1.2.1
- Match cards in reviews have a cleaner layout: the items sit on the left with the answers to place on the right, and the results list each pair on its own line, so long answers no longer overlap.

=== 1.2.0
\H2{Import from Anki}
- Bring your Anki decks across from \B{Settings → Import from Anki}, from any Anki version.
- Formatting, maths, cloze deletions, "type in the answer" cards and reversed cards come across, and sub-decks become folders.
- Images aren't supported yet: cards with images keep their text and are marked [image].

\H2{New card options}
- \B{Cloze} cards: wrap the parts to hide in \T{\\C\{...\}} and each blank becomes its own question. Blanks with the same number are hidden together, they work inside maths, and you can reveal or type the answer.
- Flip cards can be asked both ways: tick "Also ask in reverse".
- Underlined text with \T{\\U\{...\}}.

\H2{Card editor}
- A formatting toolbar above the card fields, with shortcuts: Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U, Ctrl+M, Ctrl+Shift+M and Ctrl+Shift+C.
- A \B{Formatting Guide} with examples of formatting, cloze blanks and maths next to how they look.

\H2{Reviews}
- Fully keyboard driven: Space shows the answer, ← and → mark it, number keys pick options, Enter moves on, S skips, R retries later and Esc quits.
- Match cards are answered by picking answer chips instead of drop-down menus.
- A wider card with bigger text, and maths sized to match the text around it.
- Skipping the last card ends the review, and Enter finishes from the results screen.

\H2{Also}
- Deleting a folder can also delete everything inside it.
- The LaTeX font option uses Latin Modern for card text, with bold, italic and underline all working.
- Confirmation dialogs: Enter proceeds and Esc cancels.

=== 1.1.1
\H2{macOS}
- ReviFlash runs on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and later), with the same automatic updates as Windows and Linux.

\H2{Maths}
- Maths flows inline with the text around it and wraps with it, and looks the same in the editor previews and reviews.
- \T{\$\$...\$\$} shows maths centred on its own line, while \T{\$...\$} stays inline.
- Many more LaTeX commands and matrix layouts work, such as bmatrix, cases and aligned.
- Bold and italic text with \T{\\B\{...\}} and \T{\\I\{...\}}, which also work inside maths.
- Maths that can't be shown appears as typed in reviews, with the error in the editor preview.
- A \B{LaTeX Font for Card Text} option in Settings → Appearance.

=== 1.1.0
\H2{Accounts}
- One sign-in for all online features, and you stay signed in until you sign out.
- The main menu shows who you're signed in as, and Settings has an Account section.
- Forgotten passwords can be reset with a code sent by email.

\H2{Sharing}
- Upload a deck as private, or switch your decks between public and private. Private decks are the way to move sets between your devices.
- Your cloud decks have buttons to download, update, make private or public, and delete.
- Community decks show who uploaded them and how often they've been downloaded, and you can sort by most downloaded.
- Folders come along with uploads and downloads, and are used for picking sets in the group editor.

\H2{Backups and settings}
- Backups no longer contain your sign-in, are checked before restoring, and restoring asks first.
- Deleting one deck's stats has its own window, browsed by folder.

\H2{Also}
- Updates show their download progress, and a failed update says so.
- Proper icons for ✕, ✓, + and ↔, so they look the same on every system.
- Many fixes, including unreadable Danger Zone buttons on some themes and restoring backups.

=== 1.0.0
- The first release of ReviFlash: flashcards that trust you to choose what to study.
- Flip, Type to Answer, Multiple Choice, Match and True/False cards, with LaTeX maths.
- Study Groups to review several sets together.
- Share sets online: upload your own and download community sets.
- Detailed stats with grades, session timing and charts.
- Automatic updates.
