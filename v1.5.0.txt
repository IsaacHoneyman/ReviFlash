Everything now opens inside the one ReviFlash window: dialogs appear over the page, and the set editor, settings, themes, study groups and ReviFlash Online open as pages with a Back button.
Help opens as a panel beside the page and stays usable while you edit.
Esc goes back a step on every page, and back from a page returns to wherever you came from, including the same card mid-review.
Quitting with an unsaved card in the set editor asks before discarding it.
A new default theme, Ink: warm charcoal with off-white text and one amber accent, set in Geist with Source Serif titles. Updating switches you to it once; your old theme is still in Themes.
The moving background swirl is gone.
The main menu shows folders as a row of their own above your sets, groups and notes, which now look like index cards.
Reviews set the card on the page in Source Serif, with the set's name at the top, a thin progress line, and every action pinned in a bar at the bottom so long cards scroll beneath it.
Checked match cards show each pair with a tick or cross, your wrong picks faded with the right answer under them.
The set editor picks the card type from a row of buttons, outlines the card you're editing in the list, and previews cards in the serif they're reviewed in.
Notes read like a page, set in Source Serif, with the contents as a side column.
The stats and graph view, review summary, study group editor, ReviFlash Online pages, dialogs and Help all share the new look.
Settings lists its sections down the left; the arrow keys move through sections and settings.
The themes are cut to nine dark ones round the colour wheel, each checked for readable text, with a live preview of the app beside them; the arrow keys move through them. Light themes can still be made in Your Themes.
Checkboxes, focused boxes and selections use the theme's accent instead of the system colour.
Fixed keys not reaching a page opened with a click until something on it was clicked.
